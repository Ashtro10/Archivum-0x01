Primus core visual: JARVIS Core "Nebula Fire Spirit" (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 hnturk25, see LICENSE), as bundled
with Primus (github.com/Ashtro10/astra, jarvis-main/). Files are the originals, with four changes for CLU:
- style.css: the Google Fonts @import (first line) is removed: CLU pages load nothing from other sites.
- index.html: telemetry.js is not loaded: it feeds the demo holograms random numbers, and CLU shows no made-up data.
- app.js: the rising sparks are ice and electric blue instead of orange and violet (CLU's 1.12 palette).
- app.js: inside CLU's Android app the renderer's pixel ratio is capped at 1.25 instead of 2 (mobile GPU fill rate).
CLU drives the core through window.jarvisCore (setPrimusState, setSpeakLevel, setCursorLook, setReducedMotion),
the same interface Primus uses.
