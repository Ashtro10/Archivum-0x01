Data bundled with CLU's experience kit

world.json      Country outlines from Natural Earth (public domain), via the world-atlas package 2.0.2
                (countries-50m, ISC licence, see LICENSE-world-atlas.txt), simplified by CLU
                (scratch conversion: topology decoded to rings, points closer than 0.15 degrees dropped).
gazetteer.json  Cities with at least 150,000 people plus national capitals (3,018 places) from GeoNames
                (CC BY 4.0, https://www.geonames.org), via the all-the-cities package 3.1.0
                (MIT licence, see LICENSE-all-the-cities.txt).

Online sources used through CLU when reachable: Open-Meteo (weather, CC BY 4.0, free without a key) and
OpenStreetMap tiles (© OpenStreetMap contributors, used under the OSMF tile usage policy: identified, cached,
no bulk downloading).
